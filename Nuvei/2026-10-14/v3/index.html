Policies and assumptions

The two sliders are independent. An exact rate equal to the threshold passes. At 42%, the 7/17 Fraud & Risk association fails even though it displays as 41.2%. Lowering a threshold admits weaker associations; it does not improve lead quality.

The portfolio contains 20 synthetic merchants. 17 hold Payment Acceptance; it is not universal. Region, vertical and country filters narrow displayed merchants, not the comparison base. Portfolio screens use the selected family; company profiles compare across all families already held.

Multiple source holdings can support the same target. The demo creates one recommendation per merchant and missing target, selecting the strongest qualifying source; ties use source name. Priority = attach rate +9 multi-country +5 positive sentiment (-5 negative) +5 growth or expansion, rounded and capped at 99. Recovery accounts sort last. This is an illustrative sorting rule, not a purchase probability.

Within-family products represent Nuvei capabilities drawn from the public catalogue. Their packaging, commercial independence, six family groupings and all thresholds require validation. A capability may be bundled or already included in an entitlement.

The reference ARR report sizes gaps using mean ARR of same-segment holders and distinguishes Theoretical from policy-gated Baseline. This demo shows rule-gated recommendations only: no revenue sizing, pricing model or Theoretical total is implemented.

Country expansion is an additional account action, separate from the product gap rules. Named next markets are mock planning signals, not verified demand or availability. Export downloads a local text brief; no CRM record or outreach is created.
