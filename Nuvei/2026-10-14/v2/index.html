What is real—and what is illustrative?

Representative capabilities are based on Nuvei’s public catalogue. The six family groupings, merchant holdings, countries, call excerpts, market events and priority weights are proposed demo choices—not Nuvei’s approved taxonomy or live data.

Association uses the full 17-merchant demo portfolio; display filters do not change the comparison base. The inclusive ≥ threshold and minimum base of 5 merchants are illustrative policies. Product suitability, permissions and local availability must be confirmed before outreach.

Priority = association + 9 for multi-country + 5 for positive sentiment (−5 for negative) + 5 for expansion/growth, capped at 99. It is a transparent sorting aid, not a probability or validated sales model.

Company profiles rank missing products by this score, with family name used for ties. A nonnegative expansion call or expansion market signal leads with extending existing services into new markets; target countries remain to be confirmed. Negative sentiment leads with service recovery. Company recommendations use the selected source and threshold independently of portfolio display filters.
